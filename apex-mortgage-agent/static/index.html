📰 Market Commentary — Adverse Sector

📈 Rate Direction

Following the BoE's gradual easing cycle, adverse specialist lenders have begun reducing headline rates. Precise, Kensington and Pepper have all cut by 0.15–0.25% since Q1 2026. Trackers are increasingly competitive for clients without ERC concerns.

🎯 Lender Appetite

Adverse specialist appetite remains strong. Kensington and Aldermore have loosened criteria on satisfied defaults (now considering ≤24 months vs previous ≤36 months threshold). BTL adverse criteria has tightened slightly with the EPC C requirement coming into focus.

⚠ Watch Points

Gambling flags are receiving increased scrutiny across all lender tiers — even Precise and Kensington are requesting 12-month clean periods for moderate gambling activity. Payday loan thresholds remain at 24 months for most specialist lenders.

📄 Regulatory Update

FCA Consumer Duty now fully embedded. All lenders conducting enhanced affordability checks. MCOB 11.6 stress testing requires lenders to stress at 3% above initial rate. Vulnerable customer frameworks are under active supervisory review.
